# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

safeslice is maintained by [Autometiq](https://autometiq.com/) and built with contributors from many countries, backgrounds and levels of experience. We are committed to making participation a professional, respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

This applies regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, personal appearance, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, race, religion, or any other personal characteristic.

## Expected behaviour

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not with people. Technical debate is welcome; personal criticism is not.
- **Assume good faith.** Contributors work in different time zones, first languages and levels of context. Read the most reasonable interpretation of a message before responding.
- **Give constructive feedback.** Explain the reasoning behind a review comment. "This is wrong" helps nobody; "this breaks on composite primary keys, here's why" helps everyone.
- **Accept feedback gracefully.** Review is about the work, not about you.
- **Be clear and patient.** Many contributors are not writing in their first language. Ask for clarification rather than assuming intent.
- **Take responsibility.** If you get something wrong, say so, fix it, and move on.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- **Harassment in any form.** This includes unwelcome personal attention, intimidation, stalking, deliberate disruption, and conduct a reasonable person would find offensive, degrading or hostile. It applies whether the behaviour is public or private.
- **Personal attacks**, including insults, demeaning comments, and remarks directed at someone's identity or personal characteristics.
- **Discriminatory language or conduct**, including content that demeans a person or group on the basis of any characteristic listed above.
- **Publishing private information** — such as a physical or email address, employer, or any other identifying detail — without that person's explicit permission.
- **Sustained disruption** of discussions, reviews or issues.
- **Misuse of the project** to promote unrelated commercial interests, or to solicit contributors in ways they have not invited.
- **Encouraging or endorsing** any of the above.

Professional judgement applies. Conduct does not have to appear on this list to be unacceptable.

## Security-specific expectations

safeslice handles personal data, so two additional rules apply:

- **Report masking bypasses privately**, never in a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Never paste real personal data** into an issue, pull request or discussion. Reproduce problems with a synthetic schema and fabricated values. A bug report that leaks customer data is itself an incident.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any related channels — and whenever an individual is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report concerns to **conduct@autometiq.com**.

Reports are read by the Autometiq maintainer team. You will receive an acknowledgement within **three business days** and an indication of next steps.

Please include what happened, where, roughly when, and any links or context that help. You do not need to prove your case to report something.

**Confidentiality.** We will not disclose the identity of a reporter without their consent, except where we are legally required to. If a report concerns a maintainer, that person is excluded from handling it.

**Retaliation** against anyone who reports in good faith is itself a violation of this Code, and is treated as a serious one.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and may edit or remove contributions that do not align with them, explaining the reason when they do.

Responses are proportionate to the conduct, and generally follow this progression:

| Level | Conduct | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Correction** | Unprofessional or unwelcome language or behaviour | A private written explanation of why the behaviour was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested. |
| **2. Warning** | A single serious incident, or a repeated pattern | A formal warning with defined conditions, including no interaction with the people involved for a stated period. Violating those conditions escalates to a ban. |
| **3. Temporary ban** | A serious violation, or sustained inappropriate behaviour | A time-limited ban from all project interaction. No public or private contact with the people involved during that period. |
| **4. Permanent ban** | A pattern of violation, sustained harassment, or aggression toward an individual or group | Permanent exclusion from all project spaces. |

Serious conduct may start at a higher level. Maintainers will state which level has been applied and why.

## Appeals

If you believe an enforcement decision was made in error, reply to the decision within **30 days** with your reasoning. Appeals are reviewed by a maintainer who was not involved in the original decision, where one is available. The outcome of an appeal is final.

## Maintainer accountability

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face the same consequences as any other participant, determined by Autometiq's leadership.
